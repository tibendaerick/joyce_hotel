Where Force Majeure prevents the Guest from travelling, the Guest may, on written evidence, elect a change of dates or a credit valid for months, and is otherwise subject to clause 1.3.

1.10 How refunds are paid. Refunds are paid in UGX to the original payment method or mobile money account within business days after the Hotel receives a valid Cancellation Notice or becomes obliged to refund. Refunds include a proportionate share of VAT where VAT has been collected. Bank, card and mobile money transaction charges incurred in processing a refund are deducted unless the refund arises from the Hotel’s default. No interest is payable.

1.11 Disputed payments. The Guest agrees to raise any payment query with the Hotel in writing before initiating a chargeback or reversal. The Hotel may recover from the Guest any costs reasonably incurred in responding to a chargeback that is found to be unjustified.
